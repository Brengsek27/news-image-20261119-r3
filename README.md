# Thursday 19 November 2026 — World News

A one-page broadsheet featuring five stories from the day: the global launch of *Grand Theft Auto VI*, the World Court’s Myanmar genocide case, the Trump–Xi–Putin meeting in Shenzhen, the Democratic nomination of Jeffries for Speaker, and Cyclone Akvan.

`gen.py` deterministically renders `entry.png` with PIL. `main.ts` validates the PNG and copies it to the make.fun output directory.
